<h4><strong>Chase the Run. Catch the Fun!</strong></h4>
<p><strong><em>(Update: Due to overwhelming response, registration for the TP X-Challenge Run 2026 is now closed. Thank you for your support!</em></strong>

<p><strong><em>However, even if you are not hitting the track, we would love to have you with us. Do drop by for an action-packed evening filled with electrifying activities and fantastic entertainment for everyone. Bring your friends along and let’s make it a memorable celebration together on campus!)</em></strong>
</p>
<p>This July, get ready to lace up and lock in for an adrenaline-filled experience
at the <strong>TP X-Challenge Run 2026</strong>!</p>
<p>Returning for its third edition, this event has grown into a campus-wide
flagship event that unites both our TP family and alumni, while serving
as a key platform to promote wellness, inclusivity and campus vibrancy.
On this meaningful occasion, we warmly invite your participation – whether
you are conquering the run through our scenic campus and its lush surroundings,
or simply enjoying a memorable evening as one TP family.</p>
<h4><strong>Event Details</strong></h4>

<p>🕐<strong> </strong>3.00pm onwards (Run) | 4.45pm onwards (Refreshments)
| 5.45pm onwards (Performances &amp; Fitness Stations)</p>

<h4><strong>Catch These Exciting Highlights:</strong></h4>
<p>⭐ <strong>Special Guest:</strong> Be swept away by acclaimed local band, <strong><a href="https://www.53aband.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">53A</a></strong>,
who will deliver their unique vocal magic and charming musicality!</p>
<p>⭐ <strong>Savour It All: </strong>Enjoy <strong>free carnival-style treats</strong> served
fresh at live food stations!</p>
<p>⭐ <strong>Game On:</strong> Flex your skills and take on <strong>fitness challenge stations</strong> that
will put your physical prowess to the test!</p>
<p>⭐ <strong>Win Big: </strong>Stand a chance to score exciting <strong>lucky draw prizes</strong> (for

<p>⭐ <strong>Celebrate in Unity:</strong> Join in a rousing mass sing-along

<p>Packed with boundless energy and endless fun, the <strong>TP X-Challenge Run 2026</strong> is
