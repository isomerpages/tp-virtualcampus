<h4><strong>Where Action Meets Community</strong></h4>
<p>Hey #TPFAM, this is your sign to get involved!</p>
<p>Jointly organised by <strong>Student Development &amp; Alumni Affairs (SDAA)</strong> and
supported by <strong>TP's CSR team</strong>, the inaugural <strong>Seeds of Good Fair</strong> brings
together community partners, staff and students to discover meaningful
opportunities to serve, learn, and make a positive impact. It brings together
a diverse range of <strong>social causes</strong>, <strong>service-learning opportunities</strong>, <strong>volunteering pathways</strong>,
and <strong>impact-driven experiences</strong>. Whether one is exploring
community service for the first time or looking for deeper involvement,
there will be platforms to learn, connect and contribute.</p>
<p><strong>Event Details:</strong>
</p>
<p>🗓️ 15 &amp; 16 July 2026</p>
<p>🕐 10.00am to 7.00pm</p>
<p>📍 The Plaza</p>
<p><strong>Beyond the booths, staff and students can look forward to:</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">

<p>Human Library conversations featuring inspiring lived experiences</p>
</li>

<p>Community impact workshop&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

</li>

<p>Volunteer sign-ups and service-learning pathways</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Interactive activities, performances and experiential engagements</p>
</li>
